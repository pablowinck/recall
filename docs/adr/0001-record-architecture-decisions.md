## Context

People and agents need to recover the intent behind decisions without relying on conversation history.

## Decision

Record significant choices in numbered, indexed, immutable ADRs. Human confirmation promotes Proposed to Accepted. A change of direction requires a new ADR with a supersedes reference.

## Consequences

Every structural change has reviewable context. Local refactors and small fixes do not require an ADR.
